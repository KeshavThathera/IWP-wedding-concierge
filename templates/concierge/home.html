About this concept

An independent portfolio concept exploring how an AI concierge might support a luxury wedding-planning house. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Indian Wedding Planners.

How the demo works

A Django application with a deterministic, rule-based concierge engine written in Python. No live team is involved, and all people and leads are fictional.
